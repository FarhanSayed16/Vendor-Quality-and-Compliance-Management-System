  - **Card 3 (3NF/BCNF):** White card with Deep Crimson border showing removal of transitive dependencies.
- **Bottom Callout Bar:** Crimson accent bar stating: *"Certified Zero Insertion, Update, or Deletion Anomalies."*
- **Footer:** Bottom vermillion strip with `K J Somaiya Institute of Management, India | Slide 04 | Member 2`.

### On-Slide Text & Bullet Points
```
THE RELATIONAL NORMALIZATION JOURNEY: 1NF → 2NF → 3NF / BCNF

1. FIRST NORMAL FORM (1NF) — ATOMICITY & CANDIDATE KEYS:
   • Flaw Removed: Legacy spreadsheets contained multi-valued repeating cells (e.g., listing 3 defect categories
     and 5 sample sizes in a single text column).
   • Action: Enforced atomic single-valued domains across all columns. Defined surrogate primary keys for every entity.
   • Result: Elimination of repeating groups; every row uniquely addressable.

2. SECOND NORMAL FORM (2NF) — REMOVING PARTIAL FUNCTIONAL DEPENDENCIES:
   • Flaw Removed: In a composite procurement key (PO_Number, Component_Part), non-key attributes like
     Component_Name or Standard_Cost depended only on Component_Part, violating 2NF.
   • Action: Decomposed purchase orders into `purchase_orders` (order header) and `po_line_items` (order lines).
   • Result: Non-key attributes depend strictly on the complete composite primary key.

3. THIRD NORMAL FORM (3NF) & BOYCE-CODD (BCNF) — ELIMINATING TRANSITIVE DEPENDENCIES:
   • Flaw Removed: In `shipment_receipts`, supplier contact email and contract penalty terms depended on Supplier_ID,
     which was not the primary key of the shipment table (Transitive Dependency: Receipt → Supplier → Penalty).
   • Action: Isolated vendor details into `suppliers`, contractual terms into `supplier_contracts`, and facility data into `plants`.
   • BCNF Rigor: Every functional determinant in every table is strictly a Candidate Key ($X \to Y \implies X$ is a Superkey).
```

### Verbal Presenter Script (Word-for-Word)
> *"Thank you, Member 1. Respected professors, in Slide 4 we present the mathematical core of our database: our normalization journey.*
>
> *We began by analyzing the client's legacy unnormalized spreadsheet, which suffered from severe anomalies. In First Normal Form, we eliminated repeating groups and comma-separated lists, converting every cell into an atomic scalar value and assigning surrogate primary keys.*
>
> *In Second Normal Form, we eliminated partial dependencies. When a purchase order contains multiple parts, storing component descriptions on the purchase order line creates partial dependency on just the component ID. We cleanly separated `purchase_orders` from `po_line_items`.*
>
> *Finally, in Third Normal Form and BCNF, we eradicated transitive dependencies. In the receiving log, knowing the supplier determined the contract penalty rate. If a supplier renegotiated their hourly downtime penalty, the legacy spreadsheet required updating hundreds of historical shipment rows—the textbook definition of an Update Anomaly! We extracted contract terms into `supplier_contracts`, ensuring every functional determinant is a candidate key.*
>
> *The result is a mathematically pure BCNF architecture that guarantees zero data redundancy and zero anomalies."*

### Anticipated Professor / Viva Questions & Model Answers
- **Q:** *Give a concrete example of an Insertion Anomaly that 3NF prevents in your system.*
- **A:** *If supplier contract terms were stored inside the `shipment_receipts` table, we could never register a newly contracted Tier-1 supplier until their very first shipment arrived at the dock. 3NF isolates `suppliers` and `supplier_contracts`, allowing us to register suppliers and contracts months before any physical shipment occurs.*

### On-Slide Text & Bullet Points
```
PHYSICAL SCHEMA DESIGN & DOMAIN INTEGRITY CONSTRAINTS

ENGINE INTEGRITY ENFORCEMENT (PRAGMA foreign_keys = ON):
1. Domain Integrity CHECK Constraints:
   • CHECK (defect_count <= sample_size)
     -> Prevents impossible physical inspection records (e.g., finding 250 defects in a sample of 200).
   • CHECK (compliance_rating BETWEEN 0.00 AND 5.00)
     -> Enforces strict vendor evaluation metric bounds.
   • CHECK (hourly_downtime_penalty >= 0.00 AND standard_cost > 0.00)
     -> Eliminates invalid negative monetary rates and zero-cost component errors.
   • CHECK (end_date > start_date)
     -> Prevents negative or inverted contract validity durations.

2. Referential Integrity Action Policies:
   • ON DELETE RESTRICT (Parent Protection):
     -> Applied to `suppliers`, `plants`, `components`, and `inspection_records`.
     -> A user cannot delete a supplier while active contracts, orders, or pending quarantine claims exist.
   • ON DELETE CASCADE (Child Cleanup):
     -> Applied to `po_line_items` when a draft parent `purchase_orders` is canceled.

3. Performance B-Tree Indexes (10 Dedicated Indexes):
   • `idx_receipts_lot` ON shipment_receipts(batch_lot_number) -> Sub-millisecond lot recall isolation.
   • `idx_inspections_status` ON inspection_records(inspection_status) -> Instant filtering of failed batches.
```

### Verbal Presenter Script (Word-for-Word)
> *"Thank you, Member 2. Respected evaluators, in Slide 6, we transition from conceptual models to physical database execution.*
>
> *A common flaw in junior database design is relying on frontend JavaScript or Python to validate data. In VQCMS, data integrity is guaranteed by the database engine itself using strict ANSI SQL domain constraints.*
>
> *Look at our `CHECK` constraints: `defect_count <= sample_size` makes it mathematically impossible for an inspector to enter 250 defects on a sample of 200 units. `compliance_rating BETWEEN 0 AND 5` enforces vendor rating bounds. And `end_date > start_date` prevents negative contract timelines.*
>
> *Furthermore, examine our foreign key policies. We deliberately configured `ON DELETE RESTRICT` on master tables. If a rogue user attempts to delete a supplier whose parts halted an assembly line, the engine immediately rejects the query with an integrity error. To keep lookups fast across millions of deliveries, we deployed 10 B-Tree indexes, enabling sub-millisecond retrieval of suspect lots during recall emergencies."*

### Anticipated Professor / Viva Questions & Model Answers
- **Q:** *Why did you choose `ON DELETE RESTRICT` instead of `ON DELETE SET NULL` for `supplier_id` in `inspection_records`?*
- **A:** *In automotive quality compliance, an inspection record without an identified supplier is legally worthless. Setting the supplier to NULL creates 'orphaned liability,' making it impossible to claim warranty refunds or prove compliance to IATF auditors. The record must remain strictly tied to the liable supplier.*

ADVANCED SQL ANALYTICS: WINDOW FUNCTIONS & GOVERNANCE ENGINE

QUERY 6: CRITICALITY TIER DEFECT RANKING WITH WINDOW FUNCTIONS (sql/queries.sql L149-L173)
WITH ComponentQualityMetrics AS (
    SELECT c.part_number, c.component_name, c.criticality_tier,
           SUM(ir.sample_size) AS inspected, SUM(ir.defect_count) AS defects,
           ROUND((CAST(SUM(ir.defect_count) AS REAL) / SUM(ir.sample_size)) * 1000000.0, 2) AS component_ppm
    FROM components c JOIN ... GROUP BY c.part_number, c.criticality_tier
)
SELECT part_number, component_name, criticality_tier, component_ppm,
       DENSE_RANK() OVER (PARTITION BY criticality_tier ORDER BY component_ppm DESC) AS defect_rank_within_tier
FROM ComponentQualityMetrics;

WHY DENSE_RANK() OVER (PARTITION BY criticality_tier)?
• Groups parts into safety tiers (Tier A: Critical, Tier B: Functional, Tier C: Standard).
• Seamlessly ranks worst components within each tier without collapsing rows or skipping ranks on ties.
• Instantly isolates that BorgWarner's Turbo Actuator holds Rank #1 in Tier-A Safety Critical defects.

QUERY 11: AUTOMATED SUPPLIER PROBATION & BLACKLIST ENGINE (sql/queries.sql L267-L298)

  - CASE WHEN actual_ppm > (max_allowable_ppm * 2) AND downtime_count > 0 THEN 'RECOMMEND_SUSPENSION'
  - CASE WHEN actual_ppm > max_allowable_ppm THEN 'RECOMMEND_PROBATION'
  - ELSE 'RETAIN_ACTIVE'
• Result: BorgWarner flagged for IMMEDIATE SUSPENSION; Schaeffler flagged for PROBATION; Bosch RETAINED.
